# Engagement assembly

Admirals assemble at least six strike aircraft. Routine surface encounters require an attacking force of at least 2,500 tons and a target of 1,500 tons; submarines, vulnerable repair/support forces and agreed provocations are exceptions. Only anchorage raids and sieges initiate fleet attacks on ports, with historical opening strikes retained. These thresholds are provisional.
