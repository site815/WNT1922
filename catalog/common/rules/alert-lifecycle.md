# alert lifecycle — data and balance

Decisions and major historical events open mandatory, fixed-position dispatches and pause play. Acknowledging all pending dispatches resumes play only if the dispatch interrupted a running game. Lesser notices pass once through a slow ticker; hovering holds the message. Reading is saved by the simulation worker. An ongoing battle or assault notice and its later resolution are separate read receipts. The ticker pauses behind a popup. Battle reports remain in the permanent campaign record.

The time limit below bounds optional result notices still awaiting display; mandatory dispatches are not discarded.
