# Current Unreal development checkpoint — 0.47.0-dev

Checkpoint: 3 October 2026 (Korea). This revision consolidates campaign battle reports and their 3D viewers, adds bounded representative aircraft geometry, replaces the battle sound set, and changes chart geometry updates to reduce work while zooming. Desktop/WNT1922 remains the sole source checkout. The checks below distinguish source/browser checks from the exact final native archive, its runtime verification, bounded timing samples and scoped visual review. Publication is still a separate pending gate.

- **Campaign and battle controls:** campaign navigation is numbered **01–10**. Battle reports contains the after-action report and recorded/live 3D viewer as two sections of the same encounter. Standalone historical/custom Tactical Battles remains on the main menu. Shared battle headers, control groups and SFX/volume controls give the campaign viewer and standalone mode consistent presentation; their simulation and playback clocks remain distinct. The command map and right naval outliner remain loaded behind ministry panels. Report and standalone roster rows give ship names their full width, with strength/status on the line below.
- **Chart geometry and relief:** immutable support ribbons now carry graticule, coastline, political-border and front widths through GPU extrusion instead of rebuilding their vertex/index/UV buffers during zoom. Source projection calculations and tile origins are reused, unchanged bounds are skipped, and the strategic view avoids unnecessary detailed-hull scans. Relief follows the same source heights at **1× within 100 km** of the camera and smoothly reaches **6× at 1,000 km**; normals, picking, port anchors and coast/border geometry use the matching height model. An **eight-face bounded cache** retains actual projected terrain source triangles. Hits require strict interior containment in both the geographic and rendered triangle, then use the same barycentric source-vertex interpolation with the current relief multiplier. Coast/shared-edge cases and all misses use the original land/face lookup; central-meridian or geometry changes clear the cache. The pick ray retains its 64-step march and 24-step bisection but starts above a **10,000 m × current relief** ceiling so exaggerated strategic mountains are not skipped. These are presentation changes, not new elevation measurements.
- **Battle motion and timing:** tactical playback uses deadlines so serialization/UI work counts inside the requested interval. Each advance still executes the full ten-second combat step; under load, playback slows without skipping physics or accumulating an unbounded catch-up burst. The native presentation clock is bounded by observed data and does not run ahead when packet delivery stalls. Camera subject holds and distant-action cuts preserve the actual event subject without changing simulation time or results.
- **Ports and geographic limits:** major dock anchors and names remain visible at strategic scale; naval bases appear from **4×** and minor stations from **16×**. Priority uses dated public port infrastructure; enemy garrison details are not exposed. Priority/name/actor ordering is stored once per full world packet; per-frame work is visibility, projection and label collision layout, without repeating the JSON priority sort. The bundled NOAA ETOPO elevation still samples **0.25°**, approximately 28 km at the equator, and the land mesh still samples **0.5°**. Regional relief does not establish harbor-scale terrain accuracy, and no new terrain raster or harbor reconstruction is claimed.
- **Aircraft:** six original stored GLBs provide A/B recognition liveries for fighter, dive-bomber and torpedo-bomber roles, read directly from [assets/models/aircraft/](assets/models/aircraft/README.md). They total **7,019,288 bytes**; each fighter has **14,292 triangles**, dive-bomber **13,972**, and torpedo-bomber **13,332**. Six native instance pools reserve 32 slots apiece, with a **96-visible-aircraft global cap**. A route wing shows at most **18 strike aircraft and six escorts**; a local attack run shows at most **eight**. Actual group strengths, losses and horizontal centroids remain engine records. Altitude, local spacing, bank, attack, loss and egress poses illustrate those aggregates; they do not create individual-plane simulation, extra damage or inventory changes. These are representative role models, not exact named historical aircraft reconstructions.
- **Battle audio:** eight original, entirely local stereo profiles cover broadsides, steel/explosive impacts, water columns, torpedo release, aircraft engines, structural sinking, depth charges and shell flight. They have layered transients, bass and bounded decay rather than only increased gain. Recorded source/target positions provide stereo weighting; available caliber and aircraft count affect presentation. Only observed damage creates a hit cue; explicit misses retain their recorded time, air egress duration is not mistaken for projectile flight, and shell whistles end at arrival. A same-origin worker prepares **24 cached variants**; playback uses one buffer source per voice, a compressor/soft ceiling, and a **12-voice priority limit**. SFX mute, owner cleanup, pause, seeking, quick resolution and background throttling suppress complete voices and stale queues. No downloaded sound recordings or third-party audio licenses are involved.

# Current Unreal development checkpoint — 0.46.0-dev

Checkpoint: 3 October 2026 (Korea). Historical tactical presets now default to an explicitly scripted historical mode. Denmark Strait, Midway and North Cape execute their documented sequence through the same ten-second combat engine used by free simulation and campaign combat. The desktop WNT1922 folder remains the sole source checkout.

- **Entry screen:** the static main menu separates Campaign from three historical tactical presets and Custom battle. Each card opens its matching setup; returning closes the native battle scene without creating or changing a campaign save.
- **Historical playback:** timed orders move actual hulls; salvos and aircraft travel to recorded impacts; damage, disabled machinery, withdrawal, aircraft recovery and sinking update shared combat state. Milestones carry historical clock labels and source notes. Denmark Strait includes Hood's initial mistaken targeting of Prinz Eugen and Prince of Wales withdrawing. Midway separates the initial carrier fires from later sinkings and includes Yorktown's 6–7 June epilogue. North Cape distinguishes the cruiser encounters from Duke of York's afternoon engagement. Quick resolution, watching and saved-session continuation use the same timeline.
- **Mode separation:** Free simulation and custom fleet battles remain unforced. Historical milestones do not change with the random seed; historical setup and live orders are locked. Campaign factories explicitly use simulation mode, and the script initializer rejects campaign-origin battles. Merely choosing the model catalog for a campaign cannot activate scripting. Historical scripts, engine rules and source/provenance notes reside in [combatmechanics/](combatmechanics/README.md).
- **Battle sound:** seven locally synthesized cues follow actual salvo launches, hits, misses, torpedoes, aircraft, sinkings and depth charges. The existing SFX switch and volume govern them. Stable event identities avoid duplicate sound as live recordings roll forward; pause, mute, seek, quick resolution, background throttling and controller teardown suppress stale or delayed bursts. Voices are bounded and prioritize actual damage.

## Diplomacy and economic consequences

The previous implementation treated the selected partner as access to an abstract foreign market: only the acting ministry changed. The four resource exchanges now transfer existing stocks between both governments.

| Action | Acting ministry | Other government |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sell naval equipment | Pays industry; receives gold | Pays gold; receives industry |
| Industrial cooperation | Pays gold and administrative influence; receives industry | Pays industry; receives gold |
| Buy strategic materials | Pays gold; receives strategic stock | Pays strategic stock; receives gold |
| Sell strategic materials | Pays strategic stock; receives gold | Pays gold; receives strategic stock |

Treaty-adjusted quotes apply to both sides of the same exchange. Influence is consumed domestically. Equipment sales represent stored industrial resources, not commissioned ships. These transactions change the resources available for ordinary funding, production and strategic supply, but do not change GDP/GTP directly or invent merchant delivery credit. Settlement is immediate; diplomatic shipments are not simulated as separate convoys.

AI governments retain operating reserves and decline unnecessary purchases. An AI request affecting the player's resources becomes a saved offer. Nothing is reserved or transferred before explicit acceptance. Fixed quoted terms survive treaty changes; acceptance rechecks ownership, peace, payment balances, reserve limits and cooldowns. Refusal, fourteen simulated days without a reply, and war move no resources. Three simultaneous offers and a ninety-day retry limit per proposing partner bound the queue. Offers neither pause nor open a popup, even with Autopause enabled.

Both ministries record signed gold, industry and strategic transfers. Current and previous-month values are displayed without adding them again to the resource balance. Visits and provocations remain domestic expenses and have a separate gold ledger category.
