Map

Made with Natural Earth public-domain map data: 1:50m map units 5.1.1 and 1:110m land. Commercial use, modification and redistribution are permitted. The game adds simplified geometry, approximate interwar partitions and ownership overlays. The borders are game approximations.

Earlier browser charts used the Equal Earth projection by Šavrič, Patterson and Jenny, locally implemented from the published equations. The current native map uses a fixed cylindrical chart. No United Nations map artwork or PROJ software is included.

Detailed historical ship artwork

Bismark battleship and Samidare destroyer by everlasting17th, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Original editable source meshes and textures were obtained from the AllenAI Objaverse public mirror. WNT1922 baked their scene transforms, centered and uniformly scaled the hulls, oriented their bows, merged material sections without simplification, normalized normals, corrected inconsistent face winding and removed zero-area Float32 faces and microscopic slivers whose orientation changes at native centimetre precision. All original embedded texture bytes are retained. Detailed source hashes, image hashes, changes and historical-fit limitations accompany each GLB under assets/models/ships/historical. These asset rights remain independent of the game license; no artist endorsement is implied.

Original detailed fleet reconstructions

WNT1922 authors the remaining campaign, demonstration and custom-design ship geometry and its neutral naval surface textures. These original reconstructions use documented dimensions and equipment layouts from public-domain US Navy ONI recognition manuals, NHHC/DANFS records, historical ship plans and catalog specifications. Each stored GLB has an adjacent .source.json with exact reference links, fit dates, attribution and inferred-detail limitations. No meshes or texture images from commercial naval games are used. ONI reference plates retained for authoring are US government public-domain material; URLs and checksums are in assets/models/authoring/references/sources.json. Original geometry remains subject to the project license; separately licensed artist models and CC0 texture maps retain their own terms.

Earth surface color

Blue Marble: Next Generation, September 2004, NASA Earth Observatory, by Reto Stöckli, Eric Vermote, Nazmi Saleous, Robert Simmon and David Herring (2005). Used under NASA imagery usage guidelines. The original geographic JPEG is unchanged; the game maps it onto terrain and blends local surface detail and political color. This modern land-cover background is not an interwar reconstruction.
