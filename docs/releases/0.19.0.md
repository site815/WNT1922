# WNT1922 0.19.0 — automatic operational air warfare

**[Download the portable Windows executable](https://github.com/site815/WNT1922/releases/download/v0.19.0/WNT1922-0.19.0-portable-win-x64.exe)** and double-click it. This single file includes the game, runtime, music and required libraries. No installation, administrator rights, Node.js or separate browser is needed. **Start a new campaign**; this release uses save format 11.

The download is public and can be shared directly with testers. Temporary game files are removed after normal shutdown; saves remain in `%APPDATA%\WNT1922\saves`. [SHA-256 checksum](https://github.com/site815/WNT1922/releases/download/v0.19.0/WNT1922-0.19.0-portable-win-x64.exe.sha256).

- Air attacks now have daylight/weather limits, broad search sectors, assembly, outward/return flights and rearming. CAP and strike escorts are explicit. Admirals handle all sortie decisions.
- Launched aircraft leave their hangars and remain vulnerable in flight. Surviving wings can divert if their carrier is lost. Remote strikes cannot produce gun damage on their launch carrier. Reports show air conditions, strike/escort/CAP numbers and losses.
- Carriers stand offshore for anchorage attacks. Aerial reconnaissance produces dated harbor observations; enemy ports no longer expose a live aircraft or ship inventory.
- Naval aircraft follow three-year generations, except Japan's preserved 1936 Hibari/Raiden/Tenzan/Shinden program. Carrier decks and ship floatplane stations enforce compatibility, including ferry stops. Opening wings use current types.
- Read-only government maritime patrol and anti-shipping types now have actual base aircraft, separate crews, finite stores and physical ferry/merchant reinforcements. They follow three-year generations and cannot be ordered by the player.
- All 14 starting catalogs and 28 national Markdown files are synchronized. The full review lists 624 naval and government aircraft entries across the starts.

Also includes 0.18.2's stable map-marker offsets, scrolling command list, hidden warning dates, monthly economic growth, civilian merchant growth and revised naval support catalogs.

The one-minute simulation runs in its existing worker, independently of rendering. The 100,000× setting is a ceiling and slows safely under load. Weather, aircraft statistics and timing coefficients remain explicit playtest abstractions. Night strike operations and strategic bombing are not included; the latter is documented as a proposed later government-run land-campaign system.
