## National program

The Treaty System — A historical fleet concentrated in the Mediterranean. Parity with France offers status, but aging battleships and limited funds demand careful modernization.

Opening roster from data/scenarios/campaign_1922.json and national base catalogs. Incomplete hulls remain under construction; see campaign source notes.
