Release 0.5 playable program, approved by the player. This is alternate history; class counts, engineering estimates and aircraft progression are provisional game design, not historical procurement claims. No promotional fiction is a source.

## Opening state

1 January 1936. Fast surface forces, torpedo aircraft and Mediterranean logistics. All seven national programs are active. Scored in 1950 with no forced ending. European invasion timing varies by at most 60 days; the United Kingdom and France follow the same offset.

Canonical playable roster and definitions: `data/playable/it.json`, campaign `in_good_faith_1936`. The [platform catalog](it-mare-nostrum-platform-catalog.md) contains the generated opening register and exact specifications. Merchant shipping uses the historical 1935 powered register; fleet oilers are a separate support service.

Useful historical hulls accompany the program. Explicit disposals remain in force; named surviving hulls and fleet ports are recorded in the national data. School output, aircraft output and yard expansion all use the common ministry economy, with funding between 10% and 100%.
